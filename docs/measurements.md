# .github — measurements

> The results of `.github`'s measurements: what was measured, how, on what, and the numbers that came out — trial pull requests and their runs, steps taken out of their files and run on their own, the suite of tests of the steps and its breakages, durations and sizes. The document that describes the design, [architecture.md](architecture.md), cites this file and holds no numbers of measurements itself, and a new entry of the decision log names the measurement, not its numbers; the numbers that entries older than this file hold stay in them. A measurement is cited by its number: `measurements 1.1`, and from another repository `measurements 1.1 .github`.
>
> A fact no run checks that a measurement stands on is a record of [sources.md](sources.md), and the measurement names it; documentation and code at a version or commit are a link beside it. Each measurement is a paragraph "Measured <date>:" and the items under it, and the quotes are in the language of their source. A new measurement is a record at the end of its topic, a new topic goes at the end of the file, and a record's number never changes.
